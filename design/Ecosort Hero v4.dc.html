What goes in each bin

Every item in the game belongs to one of these four bins. Learn the rule once and it works at home, at school and on the street.

Test yourself
{{ guideArt.a0 }}

Wet

Anything that rots. It becomes compost.

  • Fruit and vegetable peels
  • Leftover food
  • Used tea leaves
  • Flowers and garden leaves
{{ guideArt.a1 }}

Dry

Clean, dry things that can be recycled.

  • Plastic bottles and bags
  • Paper and cardboard
  • Glass and metal cans
  • Empty rice and milk packets
{{ guideArt.a2 }}

Sanitary

Wrap it in paper and mark it before it goes out.

  • Diapers and sanitary pads
  • Used masks and gloves
  • Bandages and cotton
  • Used tissues
{{ guideArt.a3 }}

Special care

Harmful items. Hand them to a collection point.

  • Batteries and bulbs
  • Old phones and chargers
  • Paint and chemical tins
  • Expired medicines

One class, one garden

Run a game in any period. The whole class sorts together, and the class garden shows how the habit grows week by week.

How a class game works

About five minutes, start to finish.
1
Create a game Pick a grade level and get a six-letter join code.
2
Students join They open ecosort on any phone and type the code.
3
Sort for 60 seconds Twenty everyday items, one at a time, into the four bins.
4
Talk about the misses See the items most students got wrong, with the reason for each.
5
Watch the garden grow Every correct sort adds a plant to the class garden.
